# Mixed-background scanner comparison — 110.3.32

The two device recordings show a clear capture reliability difference. In the TQL recording, the camera opens around 13 seconds, tracks the complete page and produces a page thumbnail within the next few seconds. In our recording, the outline repeatedly selects an internal table or part of the background, and no saved-page preview appears before the recording ends. A final output-quality comparison is therefore unavailable for our clip. The recordings do not expose TQL's implementation or prove which internal SDK or model it uses.

Local replay reproduced both failure classes. The old detector rejects the correct quadrilateral when the pale board weakens one edge. Strong internal table rules then outrank the complete page. Dense carpet texture also passed the old ink-density limit. Merely reducing the steady-frame timer cannot fix those errors.

The detector now includes a relative-chroma channel, uses it for both connected regions and line generation, retains low-contrast edges, and rejects dense texture. Brightness-based candidates remain available for colored forms. Edge refinement is re-scored before it can replace the original candidate, which prevents a nearby furniture edge from pulling the crop away from the page. A separate camera fix freezes the video frame before awaiting a native still, with a bounded wait and fallback to that frozen frame.

The algorithm direction is consistent with combining contours and regional contrast: [Approach for Document Detection by Contours and Contrasts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02615). This implementation is our own local JavaScript detector and introduces no scanner SDK or external image service.

Validation: eight selected recording frames show complete-page candidates with the new detector; the selected carpet-only frame is rejected. These are local diagnostic observations, not a general benchmark or physical-device timing guarantee. Private recording pixels are excluded from the repository. Procedural regressions cover a page spanning a pale board, carpet and upholstery, multiple textures and shadow strengths, internal table rules, colored forms and scenes without a document. Earlier six-background and five footer/creased-paper fixtures are also replayed against the new engine locally. Browser regression verifies the new detector in the real worker and retains the captured page when a delayed native still is followed by camera movement.
